# AngaView v2: Unified Product Vision & Architecture

**Version:** 2.0 (Unified Blueprint)  
**Platform:** AngaStack  
**Status:** Product Vision & MVP Architecture  

---

## 1. Executive Vision & Core Philosophy

AngaView is evolving from a drone imagery processing tool into an agricultural intelligence platform. The primary objective is to help farmers continuously monitor crop health and make data-driven decisions using AI, regardless of the data source.

**The Product Philosophy:**
*   **Focus on the Farm:** Farmers do not care about processing drone imagery; they care about answering, "How is my crop doing?" and "What should I do?". 
*   **Technology is Invisible:** Technology (drones, mobile cameras) is simply the method of collecting evidence. The user interacts exclusively with farms, insights, and actionable recommendations.
*   **The Living Health Record:** Every new capability or data source must strengthen the continuously evolving Farm Health Record rather than introduce isolated workflows or static reports.

## 3. Supported Observation Sources

AngaView is source-agnostic. Every data source enters through an ingestion layer before reaching a common analysis engine. For the V1 MVP, satellite ingestion is explicitly deferred to prioritize high-fidelity, actionable data collection.

*   **Mobile (Freemium Data Collection):**
    *   Captures plant photographs for immediate AI inference.  
    *   Identifies diseases, pests, nutrient deficiencies, and growth stages.  
    *   Powered by Vertex AI (Gemini 1.5 Flash) via structured JSON outputs to build the proprietary data moat.
*   **Drone (Precision Mapping):**
    *   Ingests RGB orthomosaics and multispectral imagery.  
    *   Generates field-wide crop health maps, weed pressure analysis, and stand counts.  
    *   Powered by the ephemeral photogrammetry pipeline (Cloud Run Jobs).
*   **Future Sources (Deferred):**
    *   Satellite continuous monitoring.  
    *   Weather data, soil sensors, IoT, and machinery telemetry.  

## 4. Core Domain Model & Entities

The Farm is the permanent center of the application. Everything else belongs to a farm.  

**Data Hierarchy:**
*   **Farm:** The permanent entity containing metadata, spatial boundaries, area, and centroid.  
    *   **Season:** Organizes data by growing cycles (e.g., "2026 Long Rains - Maize") to allow historical comparisons.  
        *   **Observation:** A single data collection event (mobile photo, drone survey).  
            *   **Assets:** Physical files (JPEGs, GeoTIFFs) attached to observations as evidence.  
            *   **Analyses:** Structured outputs extracted from the assets (ML labels, NDVI stats).  
            *   **Recommendations:** AngaAI-generated Action Cards.  

## 5. Information Architecture & User Experience

The farmer's workflow is heavily streamlined. The gallery concept is deprecated; images are now simply attachments inside analyses.  

### Global Navigation
*   **Home:** Provides a macro overview of all farm health (e.g., "8 Farms, 2 Need Attention").  
*   **Farms:** The primary workspace listing all registered farms and their current health status.  
*   **New Analysis:** A unified button to trigger an observation event (Select Farm → Choose Source → Collect Data).  
*   **Profile:** Account and settings management.  

### The Farm Detail Page (The Living Record)
This is the most frequently used screen, acting as a unified longitudinal timeline.  
*   **Summary:** Current health score, total area, and crop type.  
*   **Current Insights:** A checklist of immediate findings (e.g., "⚠ Early nitrogen stress").  
*   **Recommendations (Action Cards):** Prioritized, actionable advice generated by AngaAI.
*   **Contextual Maps:** Rendered dynamically to view satellite boundaries, drone index overlays, or mobile inspection drop-pins.
*   **Unified Timeline:** A chronological history merging mobile inspections and drone surveys into a single view.

## 6. Standardized Analysis Pipeline

The analysis engine does not care where the data originated. Once data passes the pre-processing stage, it is converted into a standard observation payload.

**Workflow:**
1.  **Ingestion:** Mobile JPEG or Drone TIFF is uploaded.
2.  **Processing:** Vertex AI processes the mobile image, or the AngaCloud pipeline processes the drone imagery.  
3.  **Standardization:** Outputs are converted into a standard JSON payload detailing severity, category, and visual evidence.
4.  **AngaAI Engine:** Analyzes the structured payload against the farm's history, crop type, and season.  
5.  **Farm Updated:** Action Cards and Insights are written to the Farm Health Record, alerting the user.  

## 7. Excluded / Deferred MVP Features

To maintain velocity and ensure a stable launch, the following items are explicitly excluded from the V1 MVP but remain in the long-term vision:  

*   **Satellite Ingestion Pipeline:** Deferred to avoid spatial resolution inaccuracies on micro-farms and ingestion complexity.
*   **QGroundControl Integration:** Automated mission planning via farm boundaries is reserved for V3.  
*   **Offline Mobile Sync:** Advanced caching for disconnected environments is deferred pending the stabilization of the core API.  
*   **Automatic Boundary Detection:** AI-driven field boundary suggestions from maps are deferred.
